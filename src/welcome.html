Welcome to Stales

A fast, quiet browser for Linux. Everything works from the keyboard, and everything can be changed.

Try it now: press {page:hints}, then type the letters on one of these: Arch Wiki niri Wikipedia Hacker News

Get around

  • Open a tab or search{key:new-tab}
  • Change this tab's address{key:open-location}
  • Next / previous tab{key:tab-next} {key:tab-prev}
  • Close / reopen a tab{key:close-tab} {key:reopen-tab}
  • Find in page{key:find}
  • Every shortcut{key:help}

On web pages

{vim}

Sidebar, pins and Spaces

  • Show / hide the sidebar{key:toggle-sidebar}
  • Icons only{key:compact-sidebar}
  • Pin this tab to the top{key:pin-tab}
  • New Space{key:new-space}
  • Switch Space{key:space-1} … {key:space-5}
  • Tabs on top instead{key:toggle-layout}
  • Next theme / Space colour{key:next-theme} {key:space-color}

Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it; drag it far left for icons only.

Search

Typing words instead of an address searches with {engine}.

Put a shortcut first to search somewhere else once: @g Google, @w Wikipedia, @y YouTube, @gh GitHub, @aw Arch Wiki, @aur AUR.

Privacy

{blocker}

Cookies from other sites (trackers) are refused, and WebKit's tracking prevention is on.

Make it yours

Open Settings with {key:settings} (or the gear at the top) to change the look, Spaces, search, every shortcut and privacy.

Everything is also in one text file:

{config_path}

Edit it with any editor, then restart Stales.

Open this page again any time at stales://welcome.
